      How TerraFM works

      A small multimodal vision transformer that learns the physics linking optical, radar and terrain before it ever sees a label.

      TerraScope trains and evaluates on a physically motivated simulator of Sentinel-2 / Sentinel-1 / DEM scenes. Results show the method works end to end; they are not claims about real-world accuracy. Swap in real tiles through the .npz upload or the training pipeline.
